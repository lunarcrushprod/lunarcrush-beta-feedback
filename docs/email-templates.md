# Beta Tester Invitation Emails

Pick the sender that fits the recipient, or use the variants as a base and edit.

**Subject line options** (pick one):
- `Help us test the new LunarCrush before it ships`
- `Sneak peek: new LunarCrush.com (need your eyes on it)`
- `You're invited: closed beta for new.lunarcrush.com`
- `One week. New LunarCrush. Your feedback.`

**Replace before sending:**
- `[FEEDBACK_FORM_URL]` → the deployed form URL
- `[DEADLINE_DATE]` → e.g. "Friday, June 5"

---

## Version 1 — From Jon (Head of Marketing)

Hey [First name],

We've spent the last few months rebuilding LunarCrush.com from the ground up, and it's ready for real users to break it.

You're getting early access before we open it up publicly. The new site lives at **new.lunarcrush.com** — go poke around, run your usual workflows, click everything, and tell us what you find.

What I need from you:

1. Spend 15-30 minutes using the new site this week. Try the things you'd normally do on LunarCrush — pull up your favorite tokens, check sentiment, browse topics, whatever your usual flow is.
2. Submit feedback through this form: [FEEDBACK_FORM_URL]
3. Get it back to us by **[DEADLINE_DATE]**.

Be honest. Be specific. If something's broken or worse than the current site, say so plainly — that's exactly what this round is for. Vague "looks great!" feedback doesn't help us ship something better.

You're getting this because we trust your judgment. Thanks in advance.

Jon
Head of Marketing, LunarCrush

## Version 2 — From Joe (CEO)

The site: **new.lunarcrush.com**

Joe

## Version 3 — From Dan (CTO)

[First name] —

We rebuilt LunarCrush.com. New stack, new architecture, faster everything. Before we cut over from the current site, I want a small group of trusted users to stress-test it.

**new.lunarcrush.com** is live for the beta group. Use it however you normally would. Then submit findings here: [FEEDBACK_FORM_URL]

Specifically useful to me:
- Performance issues. Slow loads, layout shifts, anything that feels janky.
- Broken flows. Things that don't work end-to-end.
- Browser/device-specific weirdness. Tell us what you were on.

Deadline: **[DEADLINE_DATE]**.

Bug reports with steps to reproduce are worth their weight in gold. Vague "felt slow" feedback is harder to act on but still send it — we'll dig in.

Thanks for taking the time.

Dan
CTO, LunarCrush

---

## Notes on the four versions

- **Jon's** is the most operationally clear — numbered steps, expectations set. Good default for the broadest audience.
- **Joe's** is the shortest and leans on relationship. Use for investors and people who'd appreciate brevity from a CEO.
- **Dan's** invites technical/bug-style feedback. Best for power users and developer-leaning testers.
- **Nico's** invites design/feel feedback. Best for design-conscious recipients and friends/family who'd respond better to a less technical framing.

You could also send the same person two versions if it makes sense (e.g. a designer friend gets Nico's, your dev friend gets Dan's).
